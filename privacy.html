Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026

This policy covers every game made by Griphos Games, including Fourfold. It explains what information the games use and why. The short version: Griphos Games runs no servers, the games have no accounts, and they do not track you.

What stays on your device

Your progress, best times, scores and settings are saved on your device only. Griphos Games never receives them. Deleting a game deletes them.

Ads

The free versions of the games may show occasional full-screen ads, provided by Google AdMob.

  • Every ad the games request is non-personalised: it is not chosen using your interests or your activity in other apps.
  • The games do not use your device's advertising identifier. On iOS they never ask for permission to track you, and on Android they do not request the advertising ID.
  • To show an ad, Google may still receive limited technical information, such as your device type, operating system, IP address (from which a rough location can be inferred) and how you interacted with the ad. Google uses this to deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices before ads are shown. If you decline, Google shows limited ads.

Google's handling of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

You can remove a game's ads with a one-time purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. Griphos Games never sees your payment details, name or email. The game only learns whether the purchase was made, and remembers that on your device.

What the games don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or other tracking tools.
  • Griphos Games doesn't collect, sell or share your personal information.

Children

The games are not directed at children under 13, and Griphos Games does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
