Fourfold Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026

Fourfold is a puzzle game made by Gamebert. This policy covers Fourfold only, and explains what information the game uses and why. The short version: Gamebert runs no servers, the game has no accounts, and it does not track you.

What stays on your device

Your progress, best times, scores and settings are saved on your device only. Gamebert never receives them. Deleting the game deletes them.

Ads

The free version of Fourfold shows occasional full-screen ads between runs in the Time and Blitz modes. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

  • Every ad the game requests is non-personalised: it is not chosen using your interests or your activity in other apps.
  • The game does not use your device's advertising identifier. On iOS it never asks for permission to track you, and on Android it does not request the advertising ID.
  • To show an ad, Google may still receive limited technical information, such as your device type, operating system, IP address (from which a rough location can be inferred) and how you interacted with the ad. Google uses this to deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices before ads are shown. If you decline, Google shows limited ads.

Google's handling of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

The remove-ads purchase

You can remove ads with a one-time purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. Gamebert never sees your payment details, name or email. The game only learns whether the purchase was made, and remembers that on your device.

What the game doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or other tracking tools.
  • Gamebert doesn't collect, sell or share your personal information.

Children

Fourfold is not directed at children under 13, and Gamebert does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
